##Pre-demo Discovery questions##




1. what is the true goal of the project you are currently working on?
2. what is the specific outcome your corporate leadership actually wants to achieve, and who is being held accountable for it? 
3. why now? what is the target date for this project, and why does that specific date matter? Is there any regulatory deadline to meet?
4. What happens if the deadline is missed? What is the actual business impact if this date isn't hit?
5. Why wouldnt you just keep things the same? 
6. What is wrong with the status quo that makes change necessary right now?
7. who else has a say in this decision? and what are the critierion that define the outcome and decision?
8. how did you buy a solution like this last time, and who else was involved in that process? (account map) navigation insights
9. what do you need to see to know how this works?
10. What specific evidence, features or outcomes must be demonstrated in this demo to prove it will work for your team?
11. what have you tried in the past to solve this, and why didn't it work?
12. what does your current tech stack look like, and what are your non-negotiable integration requirements?
13. who will be the actual day-to-day users of this solution(audience), and what are their biggest concerns about adopting a new tool?
14. How will you formally evaluate and score vendors after this demo?
15. if this demo is exatly what you need, what are the exact next steps to move towards a decision or pilot?
16. We do not entartain any back-channels or any entertainment or commissions to sell this product. Do you strictly agree with our conditions?
All our demos are transparent, we record all interactions for audit and integrity compliance. Every communication has transcribed for legal matters. Do you comply with that in writing?
